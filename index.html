FAQ

Straight answers.

Still have questions? Here are the ones we hear most.

Will callers know it's AI?

It sounds natural and friendly, so most calls just flow. If a caller asks, it tells them the truth: it's an AI assistant for your business. We never make it pretend to be a person.

My customers want a real person.

They want a fast answer and a time someone will show up. Voicemail gives them neither. If a caller asks for a person, we can set it to pass the call to you or flag it for a quick callback. You decide the rules.

What if it gets something wrong?

You get a text after every call, so you see exactly what was said and booked. If something's off, you call the customer back, and we fix its instructions. It only books inside the rules you give it.

Do I need a new number?

No. Keep the number your customers already know. You forward calls to the AI all the time, only after hours, or only when you don't pick up. We walk you through it.

Can I cancel?

Yes. There's no contract. Cancel anytime, including during your free Starter trial.
